# S1–S4: how to test

New links at the top: **Progress** and **Settings** (also `P` and `S` on the decks screen).

## S4 — Settings
- Move the **threshold** (20 strict … 80 relaxed). Study a few cards: lower = more "hesitated" verdicts.
- Untick **Looking away**, then look at the ceiling before answering: it shouldn't be given as a reason any more.
- Pulse is locked off (explained on screen).
- **Delete all data** asks for confirmation, then the decks show "30 new" again.

## S1 — Check mode (do this for the DEV post number)
1. Settings → tick **Check mode**.
2. Warm up and study. After each right answer: `Y` = I guessed, `N` = I knew it. Be honest.
3. The summary shows **"In N right answers, Second Guess agreed with you on X%"**, plus caught / missed /
   false alarms. Settings and Progress show the running total over all sessions.
4. Aim for 100+ right answers over a few sessions (Nov 19 in the plan), then put the real number in the post.

## S2 — Signal replay
On the summary, under **Signal replay**, press **Replay** on a card: a timeline of time-to-reveal vs your usual,
looking away (above the dashed line = away), blinks, breathing (shaded = nearly flat), and expression change.

## S3 — Progress
Bars for the last 14 days, "lucky guesses that became sure" (hesitated once, later answered confidently) and
"cards still shaky".

## Also new
Long questions now get a **reading allowance**, so a long question isn't flagged as a "long pause" just for its length.

## Try to break it
- Check mode with the camera off (it should simply not ask).
- Press `H` in Check mode (nothing should happen), `Esc` while the question is showing.
- Delete all data while the camera is on (the camera stops first).
