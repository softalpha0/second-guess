# Recording the demo video (≤ 2 min)

`intro.mp4` (17 s) and `outro.mp4` (22 s) are ready-made title segments. You record 4 short clips of the
real app with your real webcam, then join everything in **Clipchamp** (built into Windows 11; free).

## Before recording
- Close other apps, set the Second Guess window to a good size (maximised is fine).
- Settings: **Check mode off**, threshold 50. Pick **Web dev basics** or a deck where you know some cards and
  genuinely don't know a few.
- Good light on your face, face and shoulders in view.
- Record with **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar records the active window; press again to stop). Clips are saved in
  `Videos\Captures`. Or use OBS.

## The 4 clips
| Clip | Length | What to do | Say (optional voice-over) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Hook | ~8 s | A card you don't really know: pause, look up, then Space → `→`. Show **"Right — but you hesitated: …"** | "I got it right. But did I *know* it?" |
| 2 — Setup | ~40 s raw → speed up to ~12 s | Consent screen (scroll), tick, camera check until **All set**, warm-up 8 cards | "It asks first, never records video, and learns my normal pace in a 60-second warm-up." |
| 3 — Study | ~40 s | 3 cards you know instantly (green "you knew it"), 2 real guesses (amber, with reasons), 1 wrong (comes back "· again") | "Known instantly — spaced out. Guessed — caught, back tomorrow. Missed — again in this session." |
| 4 — Summary | ~18 s | Press Esc → summary; point at **Coming back tomorrow**; press **Replay** on a hesitated card | "Here's what it noticed while I was recalling." |

Use real reactions — don't fake hesitation for the 3 "known" cards. If a verdict comes out differently from what
you expected, that's fine: re-record or keep it honest.

## Join in Clipchamp (order and target times)
1. Clip 1 — hook (8 s)
2. `intro.mp4` (17 s)
3. Clip 2 — setup, **speed 3–4×** (12 s)
4. Clip 3 — study (40 s)
5. Clip 4 — summary + replay (18 s)
6. `outro.mp4` (22 s)

Total ≈ 117 s. If it's over 2:00, trim clip 3 or speed it up slightly. Export **1080p**, upload to YouTube as
**Unlisted**, and put the link in the DEV post and README.
