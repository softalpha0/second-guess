# Demo video script (≤ 2 min)

Record the app window only (Win + G → Xbox Game Bar, or OBS). 1280×720 or larger. Speak slowly; keep the
camera mirror out of the video if you prefer — the app never shows your video anyway.

**Before recording:** Settings → Check mode **off**, threshold 50. Study a deck once so the summary has data.
Prepare 6 cards you'll use (Web dev basics works well): 3 you know instantly, 2 you're unsure of, 1 you don't know.

| Time | Show | Say (suggestion) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | A card → you pause, look up → Space → → → **"Right — but you hesitated: long pause, looked away"** | "I got it right. But did I *know* it?" |
| 0:10–0:25 | Deck list / any card | "Flashcard apps trust your own rating. A lucky guess gets pushed to next week — and forgotten before the exam." |
| 0:25–0:40 | Consent screen (scroll), camera check reaching "All set", warm-up (speed up 4×) | "Second Guess asks first, never records video, and learns *my* normal pace in a 60-second warm-up." |
| 0:40–1:20 | Study 6 cards: 3 instant (green "you knew it"), 2 guesses (amber, reasons shown), 1 wrong (comes back "· again") | "Known instantly — spaced out. Guessed — caught, back tomorrow. Missed — again in this session." |
| 1:20–1:40 | Summary: right-but-hesitated, coming back tomorrow; press **Replay** on a hesitated card | "Here's what it noticed: a long pause, eyes away, breath held." |
| 1:40–2:00 | Settings (Check mode result, Delete all data), no-camera notice, repo URL | "Check mode: it agreed with me on [X]%. No camera? Plain flashcards. Code on GitHub." |
